Contract Risk Agent

Clause-by-clause contract review, running serverless on AWS for $0.0023 a contract.

  • Uploading a PDF to S3 triggers a Lambda running a LangGraph pipeline. Bedrock Nova Lite scores six clauses at a time against a Knowledge Base of standard terms.
  • I hand-labelled an eval set and recalibrated the prompt, lifting in-range risk scores from 57% to 86%.
  • About 10 seconds per contract, and retrieval picked the right clause type 13 of 13 times.

Boltzmann machines on quantum-annealer hardware

What if the chip's own wiring is the neural network? Scaled to a 5,280-node D-Wave Zephyr graph.

  • Visible and hidden units are assigned directly on the hardware graph instead of squeezing a standard RBM onto it.
  • I compared checkerboard, spectral, tiling and Gurobi-ILP assignments, and added label chains so one machine also classifies digits.
  • I built the FID-based evaluation pipeline, cutting each experiment cycle from 3 days to 6 hours.

PyTorch · NetworkX · Gurobi · dwave-networkx
